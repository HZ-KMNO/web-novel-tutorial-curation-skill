## Curation Rules

1. Prefer QidianClub pages that function as author-advice indexes, reposted interviews, or curated craft discussions over generic media publicity.
2. Record whether each page is an original interview, repost, forum summary, search-result-only lead, or blocked/low-text page.
3. Convert author comments into writing mechanisms:
   - reader promise
   - opening pressure
   - long-form structure
   - satisfaction design
   - character pressure
   - update/completion discipline
   - platform fit
   - prose and reading method
4. Do not copy long source text. Keep only short titles, source metadata, tags, and paraphrased lessons.
5. Keep "what this helps my fiction with" attached to every lesson. Avoid exam-style literary appreciation.
6. When a page contains links to outside sources, open those detail links only when the task explicitly asks for a deeper pass. Mark unopened outside links honestly.

## Transferable Lesson Buckets

### Self-Excitement To Reader Excitement

The author may begin from personal excitement, but a publishable web novel must translate that excitement into visible stakes, pressure, novelty, and emotional payoff.

Use for: topic selection, opening design, long-form energy checks.

### Market Reading Without Surface Copying

Read current successful web novels to learn pacing, opening promises, reader tolerance, hooks, and reward rhythm. Read classics for expression, compression, dialogue, and story-box variety. Do not confuse these two training targets.

Use for: trend research, opening revision, anti-self-indulgence checks.

### Outline As Change Control

Experienced authors vary in outlining style, but the repeated pattern is clear: long-form fiction needs phase goals, ending awareness, chapter hooks, feedback loops, and at least a record of unresolved promises.

Use for: volume planning, chapter cards, continuity tracking.

### Character As Independent Pressure System

Characters should not exist only to explain setting. They need personal desire, fear, pressure, relationship position, and action logic.

Use for: making characters vivid, preventing tool-like side characters, improving dialogue.

### Satisfaction Through Prior Pressure

Satisfaction depends on deprivation, humiliation, danger, cost, and delayed compensation. The reward should repair a wound and create a new problem.

Use for: 爽文, revenge arcs, upgrade scenes, public recognition scenes.

### Material Enters Scene Or It Does Not Count

Professional detail, news, humor, life hardship, and genre knowledge must become conflict, choice, limitation, method, or sensory reality inside a scene.

Use for: realism, occupational scenes, science-fiction/game-world settings, humor.
