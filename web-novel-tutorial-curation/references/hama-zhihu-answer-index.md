## Transferable Lessons

### Classic Reading

Read classics with a target. Useful source families include 还珠楼主, 金庸, 古龙, 梁羽生, 温瑞安, 黄易, 四大名著, `封神演义`, `镜花缘`, `济公传`, `三言二拍`, `聊斋`, Jules Verne, Mark Twain, and Alexandre Dumas. The purpose is not costume imitation; the purpose is to collect story boxes and Chinese prose force.

### Story Boxes

Recurring cores such as first encounter, apprenticeship, gaining power, revenge trigger, secret organization, rescue, public humiliation, and quest can be studied across many works and recombined.

### Satisfaction

Satisfaction depends on pressure. Reward without deprivation, humiliation, danger, or desire is weak.

### Combat

A fight is narratively strong when the reader wants the protagonist to win. Opponent setup is often more important than move choreography.

### Long-Form Planning

A long web novel needs concrete target blocks. It does not always need a full outline, but it needs phase goals and completion discipline.

### Revision

Endless early polishing does not raise ability by itself. Write forward, accumulate experience, finish arcs/books, then revise with better skill.

### Market Sense

Find the overlap between personal taste and reader demand. Read hot current works for performance patterns even when they are not personal favorites.
