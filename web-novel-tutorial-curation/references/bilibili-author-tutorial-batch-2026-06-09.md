## Topic Classification

### Systematic Story Architecture

Key source: `写作原理.docx`.

Transferable lessons:

- Separate story thinking into three scales: macro story, meso plot unit, and micro event execution.
- Macro story starts from theme/purpose, then divides the long novel into phases, basic world rules, main characters, and large direction.
- Meso plot units should be built around an emotional incident, a desire target, obstacles, methods, action/climax/turning point, and result feedback.
- Micro execution is not "write what happens." It is viewpoint choice, information exposure, scene concreteness, character motivation, emotional movement, and rhythm control.
- Reader interest comes from desire substitution, emotional experience, and expectation. A plot should trigger at least one of these clearly.

AI usage:

- Use this source to turn messy craft advice into layered checklists.
- When summarizing future theory-heavy tutorials, tag whether each claim belongs to macro planning, plot-unit design, or scene execution.
- What an AI writer can do with this: build chapter task cards that ask not only "what happens," but also "what desire, information, emotion, and rhythm does this event create?"

### Opening As Contract And Seed

Key source: `大师作品开头.docx`.

Transferable lessons:

- A strong opening tells the reader what kind of reading contract is being offered: realistic scarcity, unreliable self-narration, sensory dream logic, suspense, comedy, or another mode.
- A concrete detail can carry several jobs at once: material condition, social pressure, theme, time, dignity, and future question.
- First-person narration creates trust quickly, but that trust can be made unstable when the narrator's tone, self-image, or logic is suspect.
- Openings can encode the whole novel's grammar. If the story will use flashback, fractured perception, social humiliation, or symbolic objects, the first scene can quietly announce that method.

AI usage:

- Use this source when curating opening craft, literary case lessons, or "how to make the first page carry more work."
- Convert famous examples into abstract mechanisms; do not copy signature images, lines, or sentence rhythm.
- What an AI writer can do with this: before drafting chapter one, choose one opening contract and one detail that performs at least three functions.

### AI-Assisted Novel Production

Key source: `AI自动生成小说正文.docx`.

Transferable lessons:

- One-shot web chat is fragile for long novels because it loses continuity, resurrects dead characters, mixes worlds, and cannot carry enough selective context.
- Treat a novel as a project: story bible, genre promise, core setting, volume outline, unit outline, chapter outline, chapter draft, review, repair, continuity record.
- AI self-review is useful but not trustworthy by itself. Human reading, market feedback, and consistency checks remain necessary.
- The core loop is framework -> constrained drafting -> review -> repair -> update framework.

AI usage:

- Use this source when curating AI writing workflows, skill design, prompt injection guides, or long-form consistency methods.
- Extract workflow patterns, not model/vendor installation claims or cost claims, because those are time-sensitive.
- What an AI writer can do with this: always draft from a bounded task card and relevant context pack instead of asking for "the next 300k words."

### Ordinary Idea Expansion And Detail Deepening

Key source: `发散性思考和细节深化.docx`.

Transferable lessons:

- A mediocre idea can become a story if it gains protagonist, goal, obstacle, price, and why-now pressure.
- Do not build only forward from the first beat. Find the head and tail: inciting incident and climax/confrontation, then connect them with escalating steps.
- Branch from the object or location itself. Ask what can happen in this place, what tools exist, who interrupts, what information is missing, and what obligation changes the goal.
- Select the branch that gives the protagonist a meaningful choice, not only a louder situation.
- A good final beat often pays off an earlier detail, so callbacks should be planted during expansion, not pasted after the fact.

AI usage:

- Use this source when curating brainstorming tutorials, scene expansion, comedy/thriller escalation, or "I only have one idea" requests.
- Convert demonstration specifics into a generic expansion algorithm.
- What an AI writer can do with this: turn a single concept into a scene ladder before writing prose, then check every beat for changed pressure.

### Qidian Signing And Opening Supplement

Key source: `签约作家的个人经验与心得.docx`, treated as a near-duplicate / named alias of the 2026-06-08 signing transcript.

Transferable lessons:

- The first commercial screening window is early. The source emphasizes roughly 6000 words, then later review points, as moments where editors or readers judge whether the book has value.
- Title and synopsis should reveal genre, protagonist situation, core contradiction, and future route without literary fog.
- The first chapter should quickly orient time, place, protagonist state, crisis, advantage, and reader question.
- The first three chapters should avoid crowding. Keep the reader close to the protagonist and the main pressure.
- A golden finger or advantage is strongest when it answers an immediate crisis and promises public payoff later.

AI usage:

- Do not duplicate the same method under a new count; mark it as an alias or corroborating source.
